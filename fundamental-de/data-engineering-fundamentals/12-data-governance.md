# 12 — Data Governance

Data governance is the framework for managing data ownership, quality, access, and compliance.

## Why it matters
As organizations grow, data becomes distributed across many systems and teams. Without governance, it becomes difficult to trust or control that data.

## Core concepts
- Data ownership: who is accountable for a dataset
- Data stewardship: operational management and quality
- Metadata: information about the data
- Data catalog: searchable inventory of data assets
- Data lineage: where data comes from and how it changed
- Data discovery: finding datasets quickly
- Data classification: identifying sensitive data
- Data privacy: protecting data from misuse
- PII: personally identifiable information

## Must Know
- Ownership and stewardship
- Catalog, lineage, and privacy
- PII and access control

## Good to Know
- Classification practices
- Governance maturity

## Advanced
- Policy automation
- Data mesh governance models
