# Mock Interview

**Time**: 40–60 min. Cover all levels.

## Section 1: Basics (10–12 min) – 10 Qs
Include: DBMS vs RDBMS, Keys, Normalization (1NF–3NF), ACID, Joins.

## Section 2: Internals (10–12 min) – 8 Qs
Indexing (clustered/non-clustered, B-Tree), Transactions+WAL, MVCC, Isolation Levels.

## Section 3: Distributed/Scaling (10–12 min) – 7 Qs
Replication vs Clustering, Partitioning vs Sharding, CAP, Scaling strategies.

## Section 4: Scenario + DE (8–10 min) – 5 Qs
Real design (ecom/banking), OLTP→OLAP, feed/scaling.

## Scoring Rubric (1–10)
Clarity, Correctness, Depth, Examples, Trade-offs, Explain in interview style.

## Target: >= 8/10 overall
